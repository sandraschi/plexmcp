# PlexMCP User Instructions

When asking me to manage your Plex Media Server, please provide clear goals like:
- **"What's currently playing and on which device?"** (Uses `plex_streaming`)
- **"Find all 4K movies with a rating below 5."** (Uses `plex_search`)
- **"Fix the metadata for 'Inception'."** (Uses `plex_metadata`)
- **"Show me my top 5 most-watched libraries."** (Uses `plex_reporting`)

I can handle complex workflows. For example:
- **"Optimize all media in my '4K Movies' library and scan for updates."**
- **"Show me all users who have watched more than 10 hours of content this month."**

I will provide interactive maps and cards (Prefabs) in my responses where applicable.
